বিধবা বিবাহ

Bidhoba bibaho, "widow marriage": the opening words of his 1855 tract.

iv

The Act

Act XV of 1856

  1. 25 July 1856 "An Act to remove all legal obstacles to the marriage of Hindu Widows."
  2. Section 1 A widow's second marriage is valid, and so are its children.
  3. Section 2 On remarriage, she loses her rights in her late husband's property.

v · The second wedding

7 December 1856

Calcutta · the first widow remarriage under the Act.
